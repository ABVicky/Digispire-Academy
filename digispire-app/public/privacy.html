Privacy Policy

DIGISPIRE Academy ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal information of our students, faculty, and mobile application users. This policy outlines how our mobile application and web portal collect, use, process, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and academic information strictly necessary to facilitate student learning, credential verification, and academic management:

Account Information Student Full Name, Registered Mobile Number, Institutional Email Address, and User Role.
Academic Dossier Student ID Number, Assigned Batch, Enrolled Courses, and Syllabus Progress.
Attendance Records Check-in timestamps, module ID, date, and attendance verification status.
Course Submissions Course assignments, project files, and faculty review feedback.

2. Device Hardware Permissions & Usage

Our mobile application requests specific device hardware permissions strictly to enable essential institutional learning features:

📷 Camera Permission (CAMERA)

Used exclusively for live in-app optical scanning of classroom QR codes during attendance check-in. The camera stream processes QR codes locally in real-time. We do NOT record, save, store, or transmit photos or videos from your camera without explicit user consent.

🔔 Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used to deliver institutional broadcast announcements, schedule changes, attendance check-in confirmations, and homework deadlines. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in device settings.

📁 Storage & Files

Used only when students choose to upload coursework assignment PDFs, documents, or personal profile photos.

3. How We Use & Protect Your Information

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase) with 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES encrypted storage at rest. We utilize role-based access control (RBAC) so that student records are only accessible by authorized academy faculty and administrators.

We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Student Data Rights & Account Deletion

Under applicable data protection regulations (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act and COPPA/GDPR principles), you have the right to:

  • Request an export of your personal academic attendance and course progress records.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal or contact details.
  • Request complete account deletion and erasure of records upon course completion.

To request data deletion or account removal, please contact our academic support desk with your Student ID at the email below.
